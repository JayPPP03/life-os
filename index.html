🔬 Why every rule here works (verified sources)

Tiny starts beat motivation. Start with 2 minutes; action creates motivation, not the reverse.Atomic Habits — James Clear · Tiny Habits — BJ Fogg, Stanford Behavior Design Lab
Missing one day never breaks a habit. Habits take ~66 days to form; a single miss has no measurable effect.Lally et al. 2009, University College London
Write when + where = double the follow-through. Implementation intentions (X at Y time in Z place).Gollwitzer, American Psychologist 1999; meta-analysis Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006
Self-compassion beats self-criticism. Self-criticism predicts MORE procrastination and stress, not less.Kristin Neff (TED) + research on self-compassion and self-regulation
Exercise is antidepressant-grade. Regular activity rivals medication for mild-moderate depression/anxiety in several trials.JAMA Psychiatry trials; WHO 2020 guidelines
25 minutes a day is a real dose. WHO: 150 min/week moderate + 2 strength days. 25×7 = 175. You're above guideline.WHO 2020 physical activity guidelines
7,000–8,000 steps captures most of the benefit. The 10k figure was a 1965 pedometer ad, not science. Benefits rise to ~7.5k then plateau — 10k is a fine bonus target.Paluch et al., JAMA 2021; St-Maurice et al. 2022
Back-safe core = McGill Big 3. Curl-up, side plank, bird-dog — spine-researcher endorsed, no sit-ups needed.Stuart McGill, Back Mechanic
Walk after meals. Even 10 min of walking after eating cuts glucose spikes.DiPietro et al., Diabetes Care 2013
Sleep 7–9 hours. Poor sleep = worse focus, more hunger, higher weight gain risk.National Sleep Foundation / AASM
Deep work beats multitasking. Task-switching costs up to 40% of productive time.Cal Newport, Deep Work; Rubinstein/Meyer/Evans, APA
One priority beats five. Focus on the ONE thing; extra tasks are noise.The One Thing — Gary Keller · Essentialism — Greg McKeown
Worry-time works. Scheduling 15 min of worry reduces all-day tension.Borkovec et al., Penn State stimulus-control research
Relationships are the #1 predictor of long-term wellbeing. The engagement and wedding are not distractions from life — they ARE life.Harvard Study of Adult Development (85+ years)
Measuring itself improves behavior — proven. A meta-analysis of 138 studies (d=0.40) found self-monitoring alone changes outcomes. Your weekly time log is the highest-leverage habit here.Harkin et al. 2016, Psychological Bulletin (meta-analysis)
Action before motivation is a formal clinical treatment. Behavioral activation beat usual care and matched antidepressants in a Cochrane review of 53 studies. Waiting to feel ready is the bug; 5-minute starts are the fix.Cochrane Review 2020; Martell behavioral activation
7,000 steps captures ~90% of the benefit — pace doesn't matter. 10k is a bonus, not a pass mark.Paluch et al., JAMA Network Open 2021
Walking prevents back pain from returning. The WalkBack trial: regular walking cut recurrence risk ~28%.Lancet 2024 WalkBack trial
15 minutes after dinner is the highest-leverage walk of your day. Post-meal walks beat one long session for blood-sugar control.DiPietro et al., Diabetes Care 2013
30–60 min/week of strength = the optimal dose. Two of your 25-min home sessions hit the zone where disease risk drops 10–20%.Momma et al., Br J Sports Med 2022 (meta-analysis)
Crunches do not burn belly fat — and stress does. 6 weeks of ab work produced zero fat change in RCTs; chronic stress is an independent belly-fat driver. Sleep and calm are fat-loss tools.Vispute 2011 RCT; Epel 2000
Honesty note: the famous Buffett "25/5 two-list" story is apocryphal — the real version of that rule is Gary Keller's The One Thing. And "what gets measured gets managed" is misattributed to Deming — he called that exact idea "a costly myth." We cite what's real here.attribution research, Oct 2026

👑 How the great ones ran their days (verified, with sources)

The 3 patterns that repeat across ALL of them: 1. Sleep protected as a performance tool · 2. One deep block before noon on the single most important thing · 3. Daily movement used deliberately. Full verified file: routines.md

Steve Jobs — same simple outfit daily (decision-minimization); walking meetings; cut priorities to 3. Isaacson biography, 2011
Jeff Bezos — 8h sleep protected; no meetings before 10am; ~3 high-quality decisions per day, then stops deciding.Bezos summit interview, 2018
Virat Kohli — fitness discipline transformed his career: strict early sleep, hydration, plant-forward diet.Kohli interviews, 2019
Roger Federer — 10–12h sleep (his own casual statements); quality blocks over sheer volume.Federer interviews + Dartmouth 2024
MS Dhoni — famous 'process over outcome' calm — thin documentation, but the philosophy is his signature.press interviews 2019/2022
Haruki Murakami — 5–6 dawn writing hours, 10km run, 9pm bed. Repeatable rhythm IS the message.Novelist as a Vocation, 2022; Paris Review
Charles Darwin — two 90-min deep sessions + the Sandwalk (thinking walk).Daily Rituals, 2013, citing Francis Darwin 1887
Jocko Willink — 4:30am workout, 'Discipline Equals Freedom'.Discipline Equals Freedom, 2017
Benjamin Franklin — morning question: 'What good shall I do this day?' + evening review. (He admitted he often failed to keep it — so can you forgive yourself.)Franklin Autobiography
